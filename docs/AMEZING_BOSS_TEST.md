# Amezing Limited — Boss Test Guide

## Purpose
This test is for the Amezing Limited owner. Use fresh test data only. Do not enter real customer, sales, repair or stock records during the first walkthrough.

## Test account
- Owner/Boss account: use the real owner account only when ready.
- Apprentice test account: use a separate invited apprentice email.
- Never share the owner password with apprentices.

## Owner test
1. Sign in and confirm the shop is labelled Amezing Limited.
2. Confirm the owner can see Money, Reports, Alerts, WhatsApp, Shop Assistant and Staff.
3. Open Staff and confirm the apprentice appears with apprentice-only access.
4. Open Inventory:
   - Add a test item.
   - Receive test stock.
   - Open Stockroom.
   - Check Screen, Charging Port, Battery, Speaker / Earpiece, Camera and Back Glass shelves.
5. Open Engineer Parts and record a test part collected by an engineer.
6. Create a test repair:
   - Received → Diagnosis → Repairing → Testing → Completed.
   - Record a test payment.
7. Create a test sale and confirm stock reduces.
8. Submit a below-minimum-price test sale and confirm Boss approval is required.
9. Create a test return/refund/replacement request and approve it from the owner account.
10. Open Daily Closing and confirm recorded money and expenses appear.
11. Open Finance and confirm the owner can review money movement.
12. Open Shop Assistant and test:
   - “What changed in the shop today?”
   - “What stock should I restock first?”
   - “Which repairs need attention right now?”
13. Open WhatsApp and confirm customer communication tools load.

## Apprentice test
1. Sign in with the apprentice test account.
2. Confirm access to Customers, Repairs, Inventory, Sales, Engineer Parts and Daily Closing.
3. Confirm the apprentice cannot access Money, Reports, Alerts, Staff, WhatsApp or Shop Assistant.
4. Confirm the apprentice can receive stock and record engineer parts.
5. Confirm the apprentice cannot create/edit inventory catalogue items where owner-only control is required.
6. Confirm below-minimum sale prices require Boss approval.
7. Confirm the apprentice cannot approve returns/refunds/replacements.

## Boss feedback
After testing, collect only these questions:
- What part was confusing?
- What part saved time?
- What should be removed?
- What should be added?
- What would you expect to happen differently?
- Would you use this during a normal shop day?

## Safety
Use test names such as “TEST CUSTOMER” and items such as “TEST SCREEN”. Delete or isolate test records before real shop use.
